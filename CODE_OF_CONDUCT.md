# Community expectations

Help people contribute by being clear, respectful, and patient. Explain a
technical disagreement with evidence and focus criticism on the work.
Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual attention, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publishing someone's private information are not
acceptable in project issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project
spaces. Do not use the project to misrepresent adoption or community activity.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask someone to change their
behavior, close a disruptive discussion, or restrict participation. The
response should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Maintainers
should explain decisions when doing so does not expose private information
or create further harm.

For a moderation concern, request a private contact from the repository owner
without publishing sensitive details. For threats or harassment on GitHub,
use GitHub's reporting tools as appropriate. Security reports follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
